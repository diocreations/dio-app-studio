## What this is

You are helping test a private, unfinished version of Plattr. We are testing the
product, not you. The session takes about 45–60 minutes. You will review a
private build for your business, compare three design concepts, make at least one
business edit and one visual edit, save, leave, return and preview the customer
app.

## What we will record

- task completion, timings, mistakes and requests for help;
- your spoken comments and short quotes, with your name removed;
- screen recording **only if you tick yes below**;
- notes about your role, device and app confidence.

We will not record your password, owner key, payment information or customer
personal data. Do not enter real customer data during the session.

## How it is stored and used

Notes and recordings stay with the Plattr pilot team and are used only to improve
the product and write an internal report. Quotes may be used with an alias, not
your name. Recordings are deleted or retained according to the retention decision
recorded for the pilot. You can ask us to stop and delete the recording at any
point.
