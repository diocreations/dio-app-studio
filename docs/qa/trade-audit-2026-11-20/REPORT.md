## Known gaps this run proves

1. **No photography for hospital, plants-indoor, plants-outdoor, tuition, school, kindergarten, agency, influencer, portfolio, film, gallery, marketplace.** These trades render text-led compositions with no image boxes at all: no stock photograph stands in for the business. Premium imagery for them is a content task (licensed photography through the existing `docs/PHOTOGRAPHY.md` pipeline), not a code change.
2. **Data models are still catalogue-shaped.** Hotel nights, class places, job records, sessions, enrolments and vendors have no entity yet; those trades stop at the catalogue.
3. **Workflow-screen defects** (hotel has no nights, film books a cast member, gallery books an artwork, school books a club) are listed in VISUAL_REVIEW.md; they need the trade data models in docs/TRADE_DATA_MODELS.md, not a styling change.
4. **A customer render omits sample people by design** (`team`, `staff`), so blocks built on them are full in the editor and absent for a customer. That is intentional and tested.

## What this does not prove

- Design quality by implication. Structure, type and spacing are measured here; the visual judgement of all 27 home screens and all 27 workflow screens is in VISUAL_REVIEW.md, with its own defect list.
- Live URL discovery. No external site was crawled in this run.
- That 27 documents equal 27 finished products. They do not: a trade passes here when it has its own composition, its screens, its controls and no fake art — not when it is complete.
